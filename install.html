Get TurfDiary

Your lawn’s diary. Log yard work, products, costs and progress photos.

Open TurfDiary
  1. Open the link in SafariGo to turfdiary.github.io in Safari. It’s the most reliable browser for this.
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (top on iPad).
  3. Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll down the menu to find it. Not there? Tap Edit Actions at the bottom to add it.
  4. Tap AddKeep the name TurfDiary. The grass-and-diary icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open it from the iconAlways use the home screen icon, not Safari. It runs full screen and works offline.
  6. Allow access when askedWeather and lawn measuring ask for your location. Photos and barcode scanning ask for the camera.
  1. Open the link in ChromeGo to turfdiary.github.io in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menuThe three dots at the top right of Chrome.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install appThen tap Install or Add to confirm.
  4. Open it from the iconIt runs full screen like any other app and works offline.
  5. Allow access when askedWeather and lawn measuring ask for your location. Photos and barcode scanning ask for the camera.

Good to know

  • Your log, photos and lawn size stay on your phone. Nobody else can see them.
  • Save a backup now and then in Settings → Backup, especially before switching phones.
  • Updates arrive automatically the next time you open the app.
  • Reminder notifications are coming with the app store versions. Until then, check Coming up on the Home screen.
