TurfDiary privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

TurfDiary is made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter in TurfDiary, including your work log, costs, reminders, notes, soil temperatures and photos, is saved only on your phone. It is not sent to the developer or to any server, and the developer cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted unless you saved a backup file.

Location and weather

If you turn on weather, TurfDiary asks for your phone’s location once. The location is rounded to within about a mile, saved only on your phone, and sent only to the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov) to get your local forecast. The developer never receives your location. You can turn weather off at any time in Settings.

Lawn measuring

If you use Measure on map, TurfDiary loads aerial photos of the area you’re viewing from the U.S. Geological Survey (nationalmap.gov). Those requests show the USGS which map area is on screen. The outline you trace and your lawn size are saved only on your phone.

Camera and photos

If you add photos, the app uses your camera or photo gallery only when you tap a photo button. Photos are stored on your phone and are never uploaded. When you scan a barcode, the camera image is read on your phone to find the number and is not saved or sent anywhere. When you scan a receipt, the text is read on your phone and the receipt photo is saved only with your expenses on your phone. The first receipt scan downloads the free text-reading tool; your receipt is never uploaded.

App lock and backups

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored only on your phone, in scrambled (hashed) form, and is never sent anywhere. Fingerprint or face unlock, where available, is handled by your phone and TurfDiary never sees that data. Backup files are saved only where you choose. If you add a password, the backup is encrypted on your phone before it is saved, and it can’t be opened without that password.

Advertising and tracking

TurfDiary has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

TurfDiary is not directed at children under 13.
