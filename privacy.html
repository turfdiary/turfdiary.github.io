Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter in TurfDiary, including your work log, costs, reminders, notes, soil temperatures and photos, is saved only on your phone. Camera and photos

If you add photos, the app uses your camera or photo gallery only when you tap a photo button. Photos are stored on your phone and are never uploaded.

Advertising and tracking

TurfDiary has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
